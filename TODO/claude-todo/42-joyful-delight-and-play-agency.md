## Goal

Make Thingtime warmly playful without making delight a condition for
understanding, access, privacy, purchase, sharing, notifications, or continued
use. A person can predict the factual outcome, control every decorative layer,
stop easily, and receive the same capability with motion and flourish removed.

This epic authorizes no implementation, telemetry, production experiment,
streak, reward economy, sound, haptic, public milestone, child-directed
experience, personalization, AI generation, or sensitive-context celebration.

## Problem

Thingtime's current repository contains a shared confetti path, motion controls, Lopu messages,
rainbow design tokens, and several easter eggs. TODO 10 also holds many good
ideas, but it mixes decoration, hidden interactions, social growth, developer
tools, and milestone rewards without one execution contract.

Code presence does not prove preference fidelity, deterministic behavior,
complete cleanup, cultural fit, or non-coercion. There is no canonical mapping
from a truthful outcome to an allowed celebration, serious-context exclusion,
fallback, cooldown, owner, review date, and regression suite.

## Phase 0 — Approve meaning before effects

- [ ] Define functional feedback, decoration, celebration, surprise, play,
      reward, easter egg, milestone, serious context, and delight recipe.
- [ ] Approve the denylist: fake urgency, streak loss, variable entitlement,
      scarcity, social comparison, growth conditions, inaccessible-only
      meaning, public disclosure, and attention or emotion analytics.
- [ ] Decide which failure, moderation, recovery, security, payment, intimate,
      grief, health, and safety contexts are always neutral.
- [ ] Approve precedence and behavior for OS reduced motion, Thingtime motion,
      sound, haptics, intensity, repetition, surprise, and local preview state.
      Recommend no sound or haptics in v1.
- [ ] Name product, design, accessibility, cultural/language, privacy, safety,
      reliability, incident, removal, and manual-stop owners.
- [ ] Record durable architecture or preference choices in `DECISIONS.md` only
      after approval.

**Phase gate:** no implementation or participant intake until the charter,
denylist, contexts, preferences, owners, review date, and stop conditions are
approved.

## Phase 1 — Prove the current delight surface

- [ ] Inventory every confetti, rainbow flash, decorative loop, emoji splash,
      Lopu flourish, easter egg, rare icon, seasonal behavior, and native-shell
      variant.
- [ ] Record trigger, canonical dependency, audience, duration, input capture,
      preference gate, random/deterministic behavior, cleanup, owner, and test
      status for each effect.
- [ ] Exercise rapid triggers, duplicate mounts, route changes, editors/forms,
      account switching, failure, offline state, and teardown.
- [ ] Test current OS preference changes without reload and distinguish theme
      motion from functional transitions.
- [ ] Replace stale “shipped” assumptions with separate repository-present,
      automated, previewed, and production-accepted evidence.
- [ ] Preserve discrepancies as evidence; do not fix product code in this
      planning phase.

## Phase 2 — Specify one recipe contract

- [ ] Define a versioned data-only recipe with id, class, truthful trigger,
      allowed/denied contexts, factual message, optional channels, preference
      behavior, limits, cooldown, cleanup, owner, review date, and tests.
- [ ] Bind any automatic trigger to canonical completed state. Optimistic,
      queued, sent, displayed, read, partial, failed, reverted, or uncertain
      states cannot masquerade as completion.
- [ ] Reuse Lopu, the theme tokens, and the one celebration bus. Do not create
      a second notification, history, analytics, or event system.
- [ ] Require functional equivalence and a neutral presentation when all
      decoration is absent.
- [ ] Keep randomness non-semantic; use deterministic wording and replay when
      evidence, support, snapshots, accessibility, or fairness depend on it.
- [ ] Add validation that rejects unknown classes, missing owners, unbounded
      intensity, forbidden triggers, and absent preference/fallback rules.

## Phase 3 — Add the local explicit preview

- [ ] Create one design-system-only preview for a fictional completed private
      Thing using synthetic text and no account data.
- [ ] Require an explicit “Preview celebration” action; connect no production
      writer or automatic milestone.
- [ ] Keep one factual Lopu message identical across neutral, full-motion,
      reduced-motion, and Thingtime-motion-off states.
- [ ] Layer optional confetti only when current preferences allow it; provide
      replay-once, dismiss, disable-decoration, return, and leave controls.
- [ ] Add focused tests for event payload bounds, duplicate listeners, timers,
      particle cleanup, preference changes, route teardown, and neutral fallback.
- [ ] Verify keyboard, screen reader, touch, 400% zoom/reflow, contrast,
      color-independent meaning, reduced motion, slow device, and failure state.
- [ ] Emit no server event and retain no content, reaction, dwell, cursor,
      emotion, or preference history.

## Phase 4 — Review language, culture, and non-coercion

- [ ] Review prominence and copy so dismiss, disable, neutral, and leave are
      not hidden, shamed, or made harder than replay.
- [ ] Review emoji, color, magic language, praise, humor, seasonality, and
      surprise for the approved locales and accessibility profiles.
- [ ] Prove localization may replace or omit flourish without changing state,
      ids, authority, or factual meaning.
- [ ] Suppress celebration for pending, partial, failed, reverted, moderated,
      blocked, disputed, and uncertain outcomes.
- [ ] Delete the recipe and assets in a test build; verify the underlying
      action, status, Lopu feedback, route, and preferences remain intact.

## Phase 5 — Run the bounded private pilot

- [ ] Use consenting adults, a local design-system preview, and synthetic text;
      collect no real account, content, relationship, payment, identity,
      accessibility, health, safety, or cultural-profile data.
- [ ] Compare neutral, full-motion, OS-reduced-motion, and Thingtime-motion-off
      states and change preference while the preview is open.
- [ ] Exercise keyboard, screen reader, touch, narrow viewport, zoom, slow
      device, repeated trigger, duplicate mount, route change, and cleanup.
- [ ] Ask only whether the factual outcome, optional layer, controls, replay
      limit, and exit are understood; infer no emotion, diagnosis, age, or
      vulnerability.
- [ ] Retain negative, indifferent, confused, stopped, and no-decoration
      outcomes; do not optimize toward replay or enthusiasm.
- [ ] Remove fixtures and publish a bounded report with cohort, contract,
      environments, failures, exclusions, limitations, and cleanup receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Functional outcome, uncertainty, focus, and recovery remain equally
      understandable with every decorative channel removed.
- [ ] Every effect has one approved recipe, owner, trigger, context boundary,
      preference rule, limit, cleanup path, review date, and focused test.
- [ ] Current OS and Thingtime preferences win at presentation time, including
      changes made without reloading.
- [ ] Pending, partial, failed, reverted, serious, blocked, or uncertain states
      never receive a misleading celebration.
- [ ] No delight path changes authority, data, audience, navigation, payment,
      notifications, ranking, or entitlement without a separate explicit action.
- [ ] The preview passes exact-head unit, accessibility, localization,
      privacy/security, performance, failure/recovery, and desktop/mobile
      browser checks.
- [ ] Evaluation values comprehension, preference fidelity, functional
      equivalence, stopping, trigger truth, and cleanup—not engagement.

## Stop conditions

- An effect changes meaning, state, authority, audience, navigation, payment,
  entitlement, notification, or data collection without a separate action.
- A celebration appears before canonical completion or masks pending, partial,
  failed, reverted, disputed, or uncertain state.
- Motion, sound, haptic, flash, repetition, focus, or input capture ignores a
  current preference or blocks access, reading, escape, or recovery.
- Delight is conditioned on sharing, inviting, purchase, subscription,
  notification opt-in, data grant, streak, scarcity, social comparison, or
  continued use.
- A hidden interaction runs privileged behavior, captures sensitive input,
  reveals private activity, or cannot be removed independently.
- Logs, metrics, analytics, support, screenshots, training, ranking, or ads
  receive person-level delight, reaction, emotion, accessibility, or behavior
  data.
- Copy or presentation is materially confusing, patronizing, exclusionary,
  culturally unsafe, or inappropriate to the context.
- Any approved accessibility, privacy, security, safety, reliability,
  localization, incident, remedy, cleanup, or deletion gate fails.

## Explicit non-goals

- Streaks, daily rewards, loot boxes, randomized entitlements, scarcity,
  countdowns, loss framing, leaderboards, social comparison, or gambling.
- Growth conditioning, attention optimization, behavioral advertising,
  emotion recognition, sentiment inference, vulnerability targeting, or AI
  personalization/generation.
- Sound, voice, haptics, camera, microphone, biometrics, or background activity
  in the first pilot.
- Public milestones, badges, rankings, identity/trust signals, moderation,
  payments, health/safety events, minors, schools, or sensitive domains.
- A second Lopu, theme, notification, event-history, telemetry, or celebration
  framework.

## First owner decision packet

Approve or revise:

1. the delight vocabulary and serious-context denylist;
2. one local explicit fictional private-Thing completion preview;
3. identical factual Lopu feedback plus optional confetti only;
4. no sound, haptics, telemetry, production trigger, or account data;
