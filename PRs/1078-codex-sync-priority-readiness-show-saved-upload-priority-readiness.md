# Show saved upload priority readiness

The installed Home backup has not demonstrated that its five saved priorities
are being prepared. This adds a supported, path-free observation to existing
saved progress: trusted adapter gate, preference count/revision, fixed legacy
validation cutoff/cursor, and the last checked candidate ordinal/sequence and
closed blocker. The scheduler records its existing bounded selection; readonly
progress reads validate that exact source revision without changing authority,
watermarks, IDs, migration or execution order. A newer pending revision cannot
reuse an older candidate identity.

A separate Settings document exposes the heading and four labels as individual
editable Thingtime blocks, preserving existing custom progress layouts. Saved,
unsupported and unmeasured observations stay explicit. Migration progress is
coalesced at 4,096 rows/completion; no polling or extra full preference searches.
The stale native upload fixture now expects the shipped 1.1.0 payload receipt.
