**Status:** 🟣 Proposed · planning only

**Grounded:** 2026-09-12, Australia/Melbourne

**Evidence:**
[Joyful delight and play agency baseline](../NOTES/joyful-delight-and-play-agency-baseline.md)

**Execution epic:**
[TODO 42 — Joyful delight and play agency](../TODO/claude-todo/42-joyful-delight-and-play-agency.md)

## Outcome

Make Thingtime feel alive, warm, surprising, and unmistakably itself while the
person remains in control of meaning, motion, sound, intensity, repetition,
privacy, and stopping. Delight should amplify a truthful moment without being
required to understand it and without becoming an engagement, purchase,
sharing, notification, or data-collection mechanism.

Start with one local, explicit, synthetic celebration preview. This roadmap
does not approve a new event store, telemetry, streak, reward economy, sound,
haptics, notification, public celebration, child-directed experience, or
production experiment.

## Principles

1. **Truth before sparkle.** Functional outcome and uncertainty remain clear
   with every decorative channel removed.
2. **Invitation before surprise.** The first pilot is explicitly triggered;
   later automatic celebration requires its own approved context.
3. **Preferences win immediately.** OS, Thingtime, accessibility, and local
   session choices govern presentation at the moment of effect.
4. **Stopping is success.** Disable, dismiss, leave, and never-again are equal
   outcomes with no penalty or persuasion.
5. **No variable reward loop.** Chance may vary harmless decoration, never
   entitlement, price, access, visibility, status, or pressure to repeat.
6. **No hidden authority.** Easter eggs do not write private data, grant access,
   bypass APIs, navigate unexpectedly, or unlock required capability.
7. **One vocabulary and bus.** Preserve Lopu, the shared celebration path, and
   canonical design tokens rather than creating per-feature spectacle systems.
8. **Joy is not engagement.** Replays, smiles, clicks, dwell, streaks, shares,
   invites, and purchases are not the outcome.

## Milestone D0 — Approve the delight charter

**Gate:** owner and qualified review; no implementation or participant intake.

- Define functional feedback, decoration, celebration, surprise, play, reward,
  easter egg, milestone, and serious/sensitive context.
- Approve a denylist: fake urgency, streak loss, variable entitlements,
  purchase/share/invite pressure, data grants, attention metrics, public
  disclosure, and inaccessible-only meaning.
- Decide which contexts are neutral-only, including failure, moderation,
  recovery, security, payment, intimate data, grief, health, and safety.
- Approve motion, sound, haptic, intensity, repetition, seasonal, language,
  and local-session preference semantics. Recommend no sound or haptics in v1.
- Name product, design, accessibility, cultural/language, privacy, safety,
  reliability, incident, removal, and manual-stop owners.

## Milestone D1 — Inventory and classify existing delight

**Gate:** every current effect has an owner, class, trigger, fallback, and test status.

- Inventory confetti callers, rainbow flashes, animated gradients, Lopu copy,
  easter eggs, rare icons, seasonal behavior, emoji splashes, and native-shell
  variants.
- For each effect, record the trigger, canonical state dependency, audience,
  duration, layer, input capture, random/deterministic behavior, preference
  gates, cleanup, and serious-context exclusion.
- Exercise duplicate mounts, rapid triggers, route transitions, editors/forms,
  account switching, offline state, failures, and teardown.
- Mark repository presence, automated coverage, previewed behavior, and
  production acceptance separately. Do not preserve “shipped” by prose alone.
- File narrowly scoped bugs for any effect that bypasses the shared bus,
  ignores a current preference, or changes functional meaning.

## Milestone D2 — Specify one delight recipe contract

**Gate:** callers and reviewers can predict effect behavior from one declarative record.

- Define a small versioned recipe vocabulary: id, class, truthful trigger,
  allowed contexts, factual message, decorative channels, preference behavior,
  intensity/count limits, cooldown, cleanup, owner, review date, and tests.
- Bind automatic celebrations to a demonstrated canonical outcome, not a
  client guess, optimistic start, transport receipt, or inferred emotion.
- Keep the recipe data-only and reuse the current Lopu and confetti paths; do
  not create a second notification or event-history service.
- Keep variation deterministic when it affects snapshots, wording, support,
  fairness, or replay. Limit randomness to non-semantic decoration.
- Require neutral fallback and complete functional equivalence with all
  decoration disabled.

## Milestone D3 — Build one local explicit preview

**Gate:** the same synthetic outcome is understandable across every preference state.

- Add a design-system-only preview for one fictional completed private Thing.
- Require an explicit “Preview celebration” action; do not infer a milestone or
  hook production completion.
- Present one factual Lopu message, then layer optional confetti only when both
  current OS and Thingtime preferences allow it.
- Provide visible neutral, full-motion, reduced-motion, and motion-off states,
  plus replay-once, dismiss, disable-decoration, and return controls.
- Add keyboard, screen-reader, touch, zoom/reflow, high contrast, color-only,
  reduced-motion, dynamic preference-change, and cleanup tests.
- Record no server event, content, reaction, dwell, emotion, or preference
  history for the preview.

## Milestone D4 — Prove non-coercion and cultural adaptability

**Gate:** reviewers can remove or translate every flourish without changing capability.

- Review visual prominence and choice symmetry so neutral and stop choices are
  not hidden, shamed, or made harder.
- Review emoji, color, praise, idiom, magic language, seasonal references, and
  humor with the target locale and accessibility profiles.
- Ensure localization can replace or omit a flourish without changing ids,
  state, authority, or the factual message.
- Exercise serious-context suppression and ambiguous outcomes. Pending,
  partial, failed, reverted, moderated, blocked, and uncertain states remain
  neutral and exact.
- Test removal of the entire recipe and its assets with no data migration,
  broken route, missing feedback, or orphaned preference.

## Milestone D5 — Run one bounded adult pilot

**Gate:** all earlier contracts, fixtures, reviewers, and stop owners are approved.

- Use consenting adults, synthetic text, the local design-system preview, and
  no real account, content, relationship, payment, safety, or identity data.
- Compare the factual message in neutral, full-motion, OS-reduced-motion, and
  Thingtime-motion-off states; change preference while the preview is open.
- Exercise keyboard, screen reader, touch, narrow viewport, zoom, slow device,
  route change, repeated trigger, duplicate mount, and cleanup.
- Ask whether participants can identify the factual outcome, decorative layer,
  preference controls, replay limit, and exit. Do not ask for diagnosis or
  infer disability, emotion, vulnerability, age, or cultural identity.
- Retain negative, indifferent, confused, stopped, and no-decoration outcomes.
- Remove all local fixtures and publish a bounded report with limitations.

## Measures

| Measure                | Candidate definition                                                                            | Guardrail                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outcome comprehension  | Participants who identify the same factual result in every presentation state                   | Decoration preference and liking are not correctness requirements.      |
| Preference fidelity    | Tested presentations matching current OS, Thingtime, and local preview choices                  | Do not record a person-level accessibility or health profile.           |
| Functional equivalence | Core task, status, focus, and recovery remain usable with decoration removed                    | No inaccessible-only status, color-only meaning, or hidden control.     |
| Stop quality           | Participants can dismiss, disable, return, or leave without penalty or repeated prompting       | Stopping and never replaying count as successful outcomes.              |
| Trigger truth          | Eligible presentations bound to the exact approved canonical outcome                            | Pending, optimistic, transport-only, and failed states never celebrate. |
| Cleanup completeness   | Recipes, listeners, timers, particles, local fixtures, and temporary assets removed as declared | Removal cannot break the underlying action or Lopu feedback.            |

No target may use replay count, smiles, clicks, dwell, streaks, shares, invites,
purchases, notification opens, or continued use.

## Continuous gate before broader work

- Re-run exact-head recipe validation, listener/timer cleanup, preference,
  keyboard/input, localization, accessibility, privacy, security, performance,
  failure/recovery, and browser checks.
- Inspect affected desktop, 390px, native-shell, reduced-motion, high-contrast,
  screen-reader, keyboard, touch, and slow-device states top-to-bottom.
- Re-review any sound, haptic, public, social, commercial, child-facing,
  personalized, AI-generated, biometric, emotional, or high-impact expansion.
- Publish no “accessible,” “safe,” “joyful,” “rewarding,” or “completed” claim
  beyond the exact tested meaning and cohort.
- Stop or remove the recipe when any TODO 42 stop condition fires.

## Explicit non-goals

- Streaks, daily rewards, loot boxes, randomized entitlements, scarcity,
  countdowns, loss framing, leaderboards, social comparison, or gambling.
- Growth experiments that condition delight on sharing, inviting, purchasing,
  subscribing, enabling notifications, granting data, or spending more time.
- Person-level delight analytics, emotion recognition, sentiment inference,
  vulnerability targeting, behavioral advertising, or AI personalization.
- Sound, voice, haptics, camera, microphone, biometric response, or background
  activity in the first pilot.
- Public milestones, profile badges, creator rankings, identity/trust signals,
  moderation outcomes, financial results, health/safety events, or minors.
- Replacing Lopu, the theme system, accessibility foundations, or the current
  celebration bus with a new parallel framework.
