# Change agency and humane product evolution roadmap

**Status:** Proposed; owner and qualified product, research, accessibility,
language, privacy, security, data, reliability, support, legal, and domain
review required before implementation or any person-facing change experiment

**Grounded:** 2026-09-15 evening, Australia/Melbourne

**Evidence:** [Change agency and humane product evolution baseline](../NOTES/change-agency-and-humane-product-evolution-baseline.md)

**Execution epic:** [TODO 48 — Change agency and humane product evolution](../TODO/claude-todo/48-change-agency-and-humane-product-evolution.md)

## Outcome

Let people understand and prepare for material Thingtime changes without
freezing improvement. Preserve established tasks, deliberate choices, data,
authority, accessibility, and meaningful exit while a change is previewed,
adopted, deferred, made compatible, corrected, rolled back, or retired.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- A diff, changelog, feature flag, preview, test, deployment, notice, dismissal,
  adoption click, or low support volume is not proof that people understood the
  change or can still complete their task.
- [TODO 24](../TODO/claude-todo/24-migration-safe-continuous-availability.md)
  remains the P0 storage/API continuity authority. Product change cannot use a
  notice or flag to excuse broken established reads or writes.
- Security, safety, legal, privacy, youth, accessibility, identity, money, and
  other high-impact changes require their qualified domain owners. This roadmap
  cannot turn a mandatory constraint into false choice.
- Prior deliberate settings and valid grants do not silently reset. Authority
  never widens through product metadata, migration, default change, or rollback.
- Compatibility, deprecation, and sunset are separate states with exact scope,
  evidence, ownership, clocks, conditions, and remedy.
- Rollback must not reverse user data, revive deleted content or revoked access,
  replay effects, or erase accountable history.
- Do not optimize adoption rate, dismissal, release speed, engagement, or
  support deflection at the expense of comprehension, continuity, agency, or
  repair.

## C0 — Assign ownership and classify changes

- Name accountable owners and backups for the product contract, research,
  accessibility/language, privacy/security, data/API, clients, reliability,
  rollout, support, evidence, communications, and manual stop.
- Inventory current change surfaces: changelog, previews, flags, settings,
  migrations, capability versions, notices, release notes, redirects, stores,
  support scripts, cached clients, and custom origins.
- Classify change families by affected meaning, reach, reversibility,
  established-task impact, prior choice, authority, data, accessibility,
  dependency, urgency, and consequence.
- Approve the first synthetic preference rename/relocation and explicitly defer
  every higher-risk family.

**Gate:** no feature framework, participant work, production flag, telemetry,
notice, deprecation, or sunset.

## C1 — Define one versioned change packet

- Specify proposal, affected contract, old/new behavior, preserved behavior,
  impact map, owner, evidence, unknowns, compatibility, choices, support,
  rollout, rollback, retirement, and refresh triggers.
- Bind the packet to exact source, build, origin, feature and data versions,
  client requirements, policy/copy versions, dependencies, and reviewer roles.
- Define approved states: proposed, under review, previewable, approved,
  scheduled, available, partly adopted, paused, rolled back, deprecated,
  sunset, retired, corrected, and archived.
- Keep accumulated decisions and events bounded and relational if implemented;
  preserve attributable corrections without an unbounded embedded ledger.
- Threat-model forged packets, stale notices, crossed accounts/origins, flag
  tampering, hidden authority expansion, downgrade abuse, rollback replay,
  redirect takeover, cache drift, and insider manipulation.

**Gate:** unknown scope, authority, data effect, compatibility, stop condition,
or evidence fails closed.

## C2 — Make preview, notice, and choice truthful

- Show what changes and stays the same, why, when, affected tasks/data/settings,
  required action, compatibility window, risks, unknowns, and valid help/exit.
- Provide a version-bound old/new task comparison rather than a promotional
  mock-up. Include error, empty, stale, offline, narrow, and assistive states.
- Offer adopt, keep-current, schedule, defer, or leave only when each action is
  genuinely supported. Explain expiry and consequences before selection.
- Preserve prior choices and cached state optimistically while rechecking fresh
  authority before adoption, migration, rollback, or destructive action.
- Make critical meaning available by keyboard, screen reader, touch, 200% zoom,
  reduced motion, narrow viewport, approved language, low bandwidth, and
  interruption recovery without relying on color, icon, hover, or sound.

**Gate:** no surprise context change, hidden material effect, coercive default,
false optionality, inaccessible meaning, or notice-only consent.

## C3 — Coexist and transition safely

- Define explicit old/new compatibility adapters, source of truth, expected
  versions, read/write behavior, redirects, capability requirements, and
  retirement prerequisites.
- Recheck account, origin, ACL, permissions, schema, data version, client,
  dependency, and selected choice at every state-changing boundary.
- Preserve established task paths and prior settings while support is declared;
  never make the old path deliberately worse to force adoption.
- Distinguish offered, previewed, selected, submitted, accepted,
  authoritative-reread-confirmed, failed, ambiguous, rolled back, and cleaned.
- Keep stale/offline clients honest and safe. Unknown outcomes preserve the
  last known-good state and require reconciliation, not guessed success.

**Gate:** old and new paths meet the same approved privacy, security,
accessibility, data, authority, and task-continuity boundaries.

## C4 — Roll back, deprecate, sunset, and remedy accountably

- Define rollback by exact code, data, queues, caches, clients, permissions,
  notices, integrations, and external effects; test partial and failed rollback.
- Declare deprecation while the path still works, with a discoverable policy,
  replacement, migration guide, support, evidence, and review date.
- Approve sunset separately using measured dependency/need evidence, meaningful
  lead time, accessible reminders, export/exit, redirect or durable read-only
  access, and an accountable exception/remedy path.
- Preserve records necessary to explain prior behavior without retaining private
  content or access beyond approved purpose and time.
- Route harms to the proper owners: product correction, accessibility repair,
  incident recovery, security/privacy response, data restore, support handoff,
  portability, or exit.

**Gate:** no stranded data, task, valid grant, assistive path, old client,
custom origin, integration, record, or person silently becomes the cost of
retirement.

## C5 — Run and evaluate one private synthetic pilot

- Use adult internal reviewers, one synthetic account, one non-sensitive
  display preference, and one exact non-production build.
- Rename and relocate the preference while preserving its stored value and
  effect; show a change packet, impact map, old/new comparison, and approved
  adopt/keep/defer paths.
- Exercise direct/stale links, old and new clients, offline return, account
  switch, prior-choice preservation, duplicate action, interrupted adoption,
  incompatible version, rollback, re-entry, retirement simulation, and cleanup.
- Validate desktop/narrow, keyboard, screen reader, zoom, reduced motion,
  language, low-bandwidth, error, support, export, and exit paths.
- Remove every fixture, packet, flag, choice, notice, cache, receipt, redirect,
  and generated artifact; retain only approved content-minimal evidence.

**Gate:** two complete runs produce zero surprise, lost choice, task break,
authority widening, data drift, inaccessible state, false status, orphan,
rollback residue, or cleanup failure.

## Measures and hard stops

Measure affected-contract completeness, old/new task success, prior-choice
preservation, comprehension of material effects, compatibility results,
accessible journey completion, reconciliation, rollback completeness, sunset
readiness, remedy completion, and cleanup. Use minimum cohorts and content-free
events; allow a no-telemetry path.

Stop for an ownerless or hidden change; undefined affected contract; false
urgency or optionality; notice after effect; inaccessible material meaning;
silent default or permission change; established-task break; incompatible data;
unbounded flag fork; stale or crossed choice; forced adoption by degradation;
unknown rollback; resurrected access/data/effect; premature sunset; stranded
need; private evidence leakage; coercive metric; missing remedy; or a claim
stronger than the exact evidence.

## Expansion gates

Approve separately for production, real participants, public telemetry,
personalized rollout, automatic adoption, irreversible change, mandatory
security/legal action, policy or terms, permissions, data model, external API,
third-party apps, mobile/native clients, another language, money, safety,
health, identity, minors, institutions, or high-impact decisions. Passing one
synthetic preference pilot grants no authority to expand.

## First owner decision packet

Approve or revise the change taxonomy; owners and stop authority; affected-
contract and packet vocabulary; first preference pilot; preview/notice/choice
rules; prior-setting preservation; compatibility and stale-client bounds;
accessibility/language profiles; rollout, rollback, deprecation, sunset,
support, exit, evidence, measures, retention, cleanup, and hard-zero failures.
