# AI Idlings

Speculative product, architecture, and world-building notes for Thingtime.

For practical multi-agent setup advice, see `../AI_Advice.md`.

These are intentionally not commitments or roadmap tickets. They are idle
thinking snapshots: ideas that may be useful later when deciding what to build,
what to ignore, and what shape Thingtime seems to want.

Conventions:

- Use timestamped folders: `YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS-ZONE/`.
- Keep notes short enough to scan.
- Prefer concrete next moves over vague ambition.
- Preserve the Thingtime fundamentals: API-first data access, single sources of
  truth, test/live cohesion, security by default, and Lopu as the user-facing
  voice.
