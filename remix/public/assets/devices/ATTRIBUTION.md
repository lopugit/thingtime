# Device artwork

The model photos are representative, not photographs of the owner's specific
device or a claim about its finish. Unknown models use a generic icon. Serial
numbers are never submitted to an image service.

- `iphone-15-pro-max.png`: Ka Kit Pang, [iPhone 15 pro max.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IPhone_15_pro_max.png), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Downloaded unchanged from Wikimedia Commons on 6 November 2026; the Commons image is an extracted version of the author's photograph.
- `macbook-pro-14-m3.jpg`: Kyu3a, cropped by TenthAvenueFreezeOut, [M3 Macbook Pro 14 inch Space Grey model (cropped).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:M3_Macbook_Pro_14_inch_Space_Grey_model_(cropped).jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Downloaded unchanged from Wikimedia Commons on 6 November 2026. [Apple's model guide](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108052) identifies Mac15,3/6/8/10 as the 14-inch November 2023 family; this catalog matches the family, not a specific finish or chip configuration.
- `location-marker-frame.png`: generated with ChatGPT ImageGen for this task on 6 November 2026. The empty frame and device photo remain separate editable image blocks, with age colour supplied by the saved marker composition.
