thingtime design concept · history timeline
Unified history, made visible

Every change, in its place.

One timeline for everything you and your helpers change: each moment shows the Thing as it was, exactly what changed, and the message that explains why — whether you wrote it, Lopu did, or an app or collaborator did. Restore is never destructive: history only ever grows.

Saved on this device first, then synced to your account. Friendly words on top — Try a variation, Get latest changes, Review and combine, Send changes — real ancestry underneath.

1 · The History browser — /history

One browser, two dials. Scope says what is on the timeline: everything, one Thing, or a page and its related Things (the Components and records it renders). Look says how it is drawn: List (newest first, grouped by day), Cards (the Thing's cards over time, alternating above and below a spine that fills as you move), Line (the classic quiet timeline) or Frames (a filmstrip). Every look keeps the same filters, day scrubber and detail panel; variations are drawn as dashed lanes in every look. Click a row, card or node to inspect it; the same panel opens from any Thing's ⋯ menu.

3 · Variations — branches in friendly words

Not a separate view: a variation is a private branch from a shared starting version, and every look draws it as a dashed lane (a second row of cards, a dashed line, a second filmstrip row). Get latest changes pulls, Send changes pushes, and when the same property changed on both sides, Review and combine shows a calm three-way choice — nothing is overwritten silently. 4 · Saved here first, then everywhere

A durable local event log records every change the moment it settles — refresh, restart, offline, all fine. A sync queue ships it to your account; the pill on each row tells the truth about where it lives. Syncing a private draft never publishes it.

5 · Settings — the choices that matter

Popup and the dedicated Settings page share one History category. Safe defaults: record automatically, sync when signed in, keep forever until you say otherwise.

Record history automaticallyEvery settled change to your Things. Pause any single Thing from its ⋯ menu → History → Pause.
Sync history to my accountOff keeps history on this device only. Guest history stays local until you adopt it into an account.
Keep historyPinned and named versions always survive automatic retention. Deleting is per entry, by date, by Thing, or everything.
Show messages in the timelineNotes, Lopu explanations and app capture notes. Off shows only the change chips.
Storage12.4 MB of history · 0.3% of your account storage. Old result caches are dropped before ordered ids and messages.
Hooks into what already exists: the editor journal (editorHistory.ts — branching events with parent, time and property diffs; today in memory) becomes the durable local log; the /notifications history page pattern (cursor paging, URL filters, cache-first paint) is the list engine; the history verb joins THING_ACTIONS so PersistedThingMenu shows it everywhere; toasts route through useLopu().
Data: one owner-private timeline-event Thing per settled change (the merged Unified Timeline kind), linked by targetId, never an embedded array; local IndexedDB log + sync queue; restore and undo append events (nothing is rewritten); conflicts keep both branches and ask.
Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion / the theme's general.motion gate. Fictional data throughout.
